# Cover letters

The **Cover letters** tab holds any number of letters for the open resume. Name each after the job it's for. While that
tab shows a letter, the preview, checks, downloads and undo all follow the letter; every other tab shows the resume.

![A cover letter in the Julia design, with the same header as its resume](/screenshots/cover-letter.webp)

## Matches the resume

A letter renders with its resume's letter template, so the two always match:

- The **name, accent color, heading font and margin** come from the resume. Change them there and every letter
  restyles.
- The **body font and size** are the letter's own.

| Resume template | Letter design                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Classic         | Your name top right in the accent color, lines of your choice beneath it, then a heading with the resume's section square. Body in Libertinus Serif. |
| Julia           | The resume's header repeated: name over your header lines and contacts, a hairline with an accent diamond. Justified body, bullets hung from accent diamonds, closing signed in the name's font. |

## Writing one

- **New** starts a letter. Its header lines start with the resume's last headline part, often your location.
- **Heading**, **Greeting** and **Closing** (such as "Sincerely,"; your name is printed below it). Leave the closing
  empty to leave both off.
- The body is a list of blocks: **Paragraph**, **Subheading** and **Bullets**. Drag to reorder.
- Letters are clickable on the page like the resume. <kbd>Enter</kbd> in a paragraph starts the next one.
- **Duplicate** and **Rename** sit next to the letter picker; × deletes it (to `.trash`, so nothing is lost).

## Checks and downloads

A letter gets the resume's Parsers, Screen readers and Links [checks](./checks), and a note when it runs past one page.
Downloads are named `<name> - Cover Letter - <letter>`, in every format the resume has.

Letters are saved in [versions](./files#versions) with their resume, so you can keep exactly what you sent.
